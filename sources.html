Authority · privacy · permissions

Sources and boundaries

This is a public-safe record of what grounds the course, what has been transformed into original learning material and what remains under teacher or Community control.

Source hierarchy

The teacher-posted course comes first

The build began with the signed-in Google Classroom and its connected teacher-managed Google Drive sources.

1

Current Classroom and Drive

The teacher-posted ten-week unit program, weekly sequence, workbook intent, recipe collection and current modification evidence establish the course identity and learning flow.

2

Current school controls

The 2026 school assessment booklet controls the formal Food Technology schedule. It does not name this unit, so no archived unit task has been presented as current.

3

Official curriculum

The course is mapped to the NSW Food Technology Years 7–10 Syllabus (2019) for the 2026 default pathway.

Original public learning

What this site may do

  • Teach the verified concepts in new, student-friendly language.
  • Provide original feedback checks, evidence prompts and applied activities.
  • Link to checked public media for a named learning purpose.
  • Provide an editable, locally reviewable teacher program and presentations.

Protected material

What this site does not copy

  • Commercial professional-learning worksheets, photographs, recipes or teacher answers.
  • Teacher-only Classroom posts, Drive identifiers or access-controlled files.
  • Archived assessment notices or marking sheets as current instructions.
  • Student names, work, grades, comments, photographs or class data.

Teacher to confirm

What stays local

  • Practical recipes, timing, ingredients, allergies, equipment and supervision.
  • Assessment task, dates, weighting, submission method and marking criteria.
  • Any early use of the 2025 syllabus before statewide implementation.
  • Local delivery sequence and access to controlled Classroom or Drive materials.

Cultural authority

Living, local and owned

  • Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Cultural Knowledge is not a generic collection of facts.
  • Finding material online does not create permission to copy, adapt or perform it.
  • Local Community, AECG or Knowledge Holder guidance remains visible and necessary.
  • Sacred, restricted or uncertain material is not reproduced.
